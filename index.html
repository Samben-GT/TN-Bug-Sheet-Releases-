About

Reports go straight to the TN Bug Sheet team. If there is no signal, they wait on this phone and send by themselves later. Build .

Before you start

  • Report only public problems: roads, lights, drains, garbage, water, power.
  • Your location is attached to each report. It is used to find the right office.
  • Keep faces and number plates out of photos.
  • Do not report while driving. Stop first.
  • Photos may be checked by an AI tool to sort the bug type.
